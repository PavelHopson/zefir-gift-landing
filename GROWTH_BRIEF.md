# GROWTH_BRIEF.md

## Goal

Turn `zefir-gift-landing` from a pretty storefront into a stronger local acquisition page for the home confectionery `Vse v Zefire`.

The site should help with:
- direct orders from Telegram
- local search visibility in Kaliningrad
- better conversion from social traffic
- seasonal promo packaging
- clearer proof for higher-ticket bouquet and event orders

## Core Promise

Handmade marshmallow gifts in Kaliningrad that look premium, taste soft, and are ready to be gifted without extra effort.

## Primary Conversion

Main conversion:
- click to Telegram and start an order conversation

Secondary conversions:
- view Instagram portfolio
- inspect collection and pricing
- save the site before a holiday or event

## Audience Segments

### Segment 1: Gift buyers

Needs:
- a beautiful ready-made gift
- fast response
- confidence that the gift looks expensive

Triggers:
- birthdays
- thank-you gifts
- teacher gifts
- romantic gestures
- "need something tasteful today/tomorrow"

### Segment 2: Event customers

Needs:
- custom bouquet or edible composition
- trust in execution
- clarity on timing and packaging

Triggers:
- weddings
- anniversaries
- corporate gifts
- baby showers
- themed events

### Segment 3: Repeat local fans

Needs:
- quick reorder
- new seasonal drops
- familiar direct communication

Triggers:
- holidays
- repeat gifting
- dessert orders for family events

## SEO Direction

### Primary local intent

Highest-value clusters:
- zefir Kaliningrad
- marshmallow bouquet Kaliningrad

- handmade sweets Kaliningrad
- gift sweets Kaliningrad

### Suggested page/section themes

- edible bouquets in Kaliningrad
- handmade marshmallow gifts
- marshmallow roses and peonies
- custom gift boxes and compliments
- marshmallow mushrooms and unusual dessert gifts
- cheesecake orders in Kaliningrad
- wedding and event dessert compositions

### Content requirements

The page should repeatedly and naturally mention:
- Kaliningrad
- handmade
- delivery
- bouquet
- gift
- marshmallow / zefir

Avoid keyword stuffing.
Local trust matters more than raw keyword density.

## Offer Strategy

Use three offer layers:

### Entry offer

- compliment boxes
- small boxes of twists
- easy first purchase

Purpose:
- low-friction first order

### Signature offer

- edible bouquets
- premium compositions
- branded seasonal gift sets

Purpose:
- strongest emotional positioning

### Occasion offer

- birthdays
- weddings
- teacher gifts
- corporate gifting
- holiday collections

Purpose:
- convert intent around a specific event

## Proof Strategy

Add or strengthen:
- real customer quotes
- photo proof of delivered work
- packaging close-ups
- before-delivery previews where available
- local delivery specifics
- repeat-order language

Best proof sequence:
1. real products
2. real details
3. real reviews
4. clear ordering path

## Friction to Remove

The page should answer quickly:
- how much does it cost?
- how soon can I get it?
- how do I order?
- do you deliver in Kaliningrad?
- can you make a custom composition?
- how long does it stay fresh?

If the answer exists, it should be visible before the user needs to ask.

## Seasonal Marketing Plan

Build reusable seasonal layers for:
- 14 February

- wedding season
- autumn mushroom collections
- New Year / winter gift boxes

For each season prepare:
- hero variant
- promo strip
- 3 short-form content hooks
- 1 Telegram CTA
- 1 limited-time product group

Each product or seasonal drop should yield:
- 1 portfolio photo set
- 1 short reel
- 1 Telegram post
- 1 Instagram caption
- 1 site update
